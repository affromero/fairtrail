Flight Finder rental location catalog

Airport names, identifiers and coordinates: OurAirports, public domain.
https://ourairports.com/data/

City names, aliases, administrative regions, coordinates and city timezones:
GeoNames, Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0).
https://www.geonames.org/
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Airport timezone assignments are derived from Timezone Boundary Builder data,
copyright OpenStreetMap contributors, under the Open Database License (ODbL) 1.0.
The combined catalog is made available under ODbL 1.0, retaining the GeoNames
attribution above. The source database is catalog.json.gz beside this notice
(gzip-compressed newline-delimited JSON, one location per line). It is also
downloadable from /api/cars/location-data on a running instance.
https://github.com/evansiroky/timezone-boundary-builder
https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright
https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/

The geo-tz lookup software is MIT licensed, copyright Evan Siroky.
https://github.com/evansiroky/node-geo-tz/blob/master/LICENSE.md

Changes: selected open airports with unique IATA codes; excluded ambiguous or
ocean airport timezones; selected populated places; normalized search aliases;
retained up to 32 aliases per place; combined records into a compressed catalog.
Data is supplied without a guarantee of accuracy or completeness. A geographic
match does not establish provider availability or a rental quote.

manifest.json records exact input URLs, checksums, generator and lookup versions.
Rebuild with: node --import tsx scripts/build-car-locations.mts
Source archives are read from CAR_CATALOG_SOURCE_DIR or downloaded into the
temporary source cache. Checksums must match; changed sources fail visibly.
Use --refresh-sources only when intentionally reviewing a catalog update.
